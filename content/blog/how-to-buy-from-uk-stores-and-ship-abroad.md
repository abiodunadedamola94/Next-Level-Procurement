Lots of people outside the UK want things they can only find in UK shops. Two problems usually get in the way.

## The two usual problems

- **The shop won't ship to your country.** Many UK stores only deliver inside the UK.
- **The shop won't take your card.** Some checkouts refuse cards issued abroad.

## How a personal shopper solves both

A personal shopper in the UK buys the item for you, receives it at a UK address, and sends it on to you.

1. You tell us what you want, with a link or a photo if you have one.
2. We confirm the price and what it costs to send it to your country.
3. We buy it in the UK and check it arrived as ordered.
4. We ship it to you and keep you updated until it arrives.

## What to send us

- The item, and a link if you have one
- The size, colour or model
- How many you need
- The city and country it should go to

It works for one item for yourself or many for your business.
