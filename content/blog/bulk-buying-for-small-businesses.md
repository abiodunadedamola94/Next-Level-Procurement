Buying in larger quantities usually lowers the price of each item. A large order also ties up more money, so check a few things first.

## Before you order

- **Does it sell?** Start with products you already know your customers buy.
- **Can you store it?** Plan where the goods will go when they arrive.
- **Is the quality the same every time?** Ask for a sample and compare it with what arrives.
- **What is the full cost?** Add shipping and any other charges to the product price, so you compare like with like.
