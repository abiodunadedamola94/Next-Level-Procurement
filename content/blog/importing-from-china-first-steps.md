Importing from China can give your business better prices, but a first order has a few steps you should know about.

## 1. Know exactly what you need

Write down the product, the quantity, the quality you expect and your budget. Clear details get you clearer prices.

## 2. Find suppliers you can trust

There are many suppliers for most products. Compare more than one, and ask for photos, samples or references before you pay.

## 3. Agree the price and the terms

Agree the price, the quantity, when the goods will be ready and how payment works. Get it in writing.

## 4. Check the quality before it ships

It is much easier to fix a problem before the goods leave China than after they arrive.

## 5. Arrange shipping and delivery

Decide how the goods will travel and who handles each step until they reach you.
